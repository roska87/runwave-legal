Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

In short: today the app does not send your data to any server of ours. Your runs are read from the Health app on your devices and are not copied to iCloud. Your workouts and shoes are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The app has no ads or analytics, and does not sell or share information with third parties.

The app is in Spanish; names of buttons and settings are shown as they appear in the app, with a translation.

Who is responsible

Runwave is developed by Ari Rostkier, in Uruguay. For any privacy question, write to runwave.app.soporte@gmail.com.

Health app data

  • What it reads: with your permission, Runwave reads your runs from Health (date, duration, distance, pace, heart rate, elevation, energy, route, laps or segments, and whether it was on a treadmill) to show your history, statistics, splits and shoe mileage.
  • Where it stays: this information is processed on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. The iPhone passes a summary of your recent runs to your watch (see "Apple Watch"). It is not copied to iCloud, not sent to any server of ours and not included in the data export. The only exception is the "Fijar total" (set total) adjustment (see "What you create in the app").
  • Maps: the route is drawn on Apple Maps. To show it, the app downloads the map of that area from Apple.
  • What it writes: Runwave does not write or modify data in Health. On iPhone it only asks for permission to schedule your workouts in the Workout app on Apple Watch. On the watch it writes nothing.
  • Use: Health data is used only to provide the app's features. It is never used for advertising, sold or shared with third parties.

What you create in the app

  • Workouts, shoes, scheduled workouts and preferences (unit, weekly goal, how you arrange your statistics): stored on your device.
  • Data linked to your runs: which shoe you wore for each run, which workout it belongs to, and the name and category you give it. These are stored with the identifier Health assigns to the run, not with the run's data.
  • "Fijar total" (set total): if you type a shoe's total mileage by hand, the app stores the adjustment needed to reach that total from the mileage calculated with your runs.
  • iCloud: if you are signed in to iCloud, everything in this section syncs between your devices through your account's private iCloud database. Apple runs it and we have no access to it.

Apple Watch

  • The iPhone sends directly to your watch, between your own devices, your preferences, your shoes with their mileage, your upcoming scheduled workouts and a summary of your recent runs (date, duration, distance, average heart rate, elevation and shoe).
  • The watch sends the iPhone the shoe you choose for a run.
  • The watch keeps a temporary copy of that data and of what its complications show, so it works without the iPhone nearby. That copy is excluded from backups.

Camera and photos

If you take or choose a photo of a shoe, it is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud. The app does not ask for access to your whole photo library and does not send the photo anywhere else.

Exporting your data

From Settings ("Configuración") you can export a file with your workouts, shoes (without photos), mileage adjustments (except "Fijar total"), scheduled workouts and preferences. It does not include Health data. You decide where to save that file or whom to share it with.

Garmin (coming soon)

We are preparing the option to send your workouts to a Garmin watch. This feature is not in the app yet. When it is available:

  • It will be optional: it only works if you connect your Garmin Connect account from Runwave. You sign in on Garmin's page; Runwave never sees your password.
  • To send workouts we will use a Runwave server. On it we will store the access permission that Garmin gives us, your Garmin user identifier, an identifier of your Runwave installation (to know whom that permission belongs to) and the identifiers of the workouts Runwave published to Garmin (to replace them if you send a workout again).
  • We will only send to your Garmin Connect calendar the workouts you choose: name, steps, distances, times, target paces and, if you want, the date. They pass through our server only to be sent; we do not store them there.
  • Runwave will not read your Garmin activities or health data.
  • Garmin-related data will not be used to train artificial intelligence models and will not be shared with third parties.
  • When you disconnect Garmin in the app, we will delete everything related to your Garmin account from our server and ask Garmin to revoke the permission. If you remove access from Garmin Connect, Garmin notifies us and we delete it as well.
  • What you send to Garmin stays in your Garmin account and is governed by Garmin's privacy policy.

Before this feature reaches the app, we will update this policy with the final details, such as where the server is located and how soon data is deleted.

Tracking and third parties

Runwave does not track you across apps or websites, shows no ads, uses no third-party analytics or SDKs, and does not sell or share data. If you agreed on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports for the app.

If you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. Our support email uses Gmail, by Google. If you want us to delete your messages, just ask.

How to delete your data

  • To delete what the app stores on your device, delete the app.
  • To delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud, open your account storage, choose Runwave and delete its data.
  • Your runs stay in the Health app, where they were recorded. To remove Runwave's access, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Runwave.

Children

Runwave is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Today the app does not send us personal data, so we have no information about you to access, correct or delete, other than emails you sent us. If you have a question or request under Uruguay's Personal Data Protection Law No. 18,331, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
