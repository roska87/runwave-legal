Help and contact

A question, a bug or an idea? Write to runwave.app.soporte@gmail.com. If it's a bug, tell us what you were doing and, if you can, send a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Runwave works without sign-up. Your workouts and shoes are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud account.

Why does it ask for Health access?

To read your runs and show your history, statistics and shoe mileage. Your runs are not copied to iCloud or sent to any server of ours.

How do I send a workout to my Apple Watch?

On iPhone, open the workout in "Library" and tap "Send to Apple Watch". You can add it to the Workout app on your watch or schedule it for the day and time you choose.

How do I add miles to a shoe?

Open the run in "History" and choose the shoe. If you wore the pair without recording the run, open the shoe and tap "Adjust Mileage".

Can I have the same data on iPhone and iPad?

Yes, if both are signed in to the same iCloud account. To see your runs on iPad, also turn on Health in iCloud on both devices.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove what is stored on the device. For the iCloud copy and Health access, follow the steps in the privacy policy.
