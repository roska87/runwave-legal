Your workouts, on your wrist

Runwave is a running app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. You build workouts step by step (warm-up, intervals, recoveries and cool-down, with distance, time and target pace) and send them to your watch so it guides you while you run.

It also tracks the mileage of your running shoes, shows your run history and shows your statistics, based on the runs you already have in the Health app.

Runwave is available in English and Spanish.

No sign-up needed: your workouts and shoes are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. Only Groups, which is optional and lets you share workouts with your team or your athletes, asks you to sign in with Apple.
